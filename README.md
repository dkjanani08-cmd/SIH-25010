## Problem Title
SIH 25010: Smart Crop Advisory System for Small and Marginal Farmers
## Problem Description
A majority of small and marginal farmers in India rely on traditional knowledge, local shopkeepers, or guesswork for crop selection, pest control, and fertilizer use. They lack access to personalized, real-time advisory services that account for soil type, weather conditions, and crop history. This often leads to poor yield, excessive input costs, and environmental degradation due to overuse of chemicals. Language barriers, low digital literacy, and absence of localized tools further limit their access to modern agri-tech resources.

Impact / Why this problem needs to be solved

Helping small farmers make informed decisions can significantly increase productivity, reduce costs, and improve livelihoods. It also contributes to sustainable farming practices, food security, and environmental conservation. A smart advisory solution can empower farmers with scientific insights in their native language and reduce dependency on unreliable third-party advice.

Expected Outcomes

• A multilingual, AI-based mobile app or chatbot that provides real-time, location-specific crop advisory.
• Soil health recommendations and fertilizer guidance.
• Weather-based alerts and predictive insights.
• Pest/disease detection via image uploads.
• Market price tracking.
• Voice support for low-literate users.
• Feedback and usage data collection for continuous improvement.

Relevant Stakeholders / Beneficiaries

• Small and marginal farmers
• Agricultural extension officers
• Government agriculture departments
• NGOs and cooperatives
• Agri-tech startups

Supporting Data

• 86% of Indian farmers are small or marginal (NABARD Report, 2022).
• Studies show ICT-based advisories can increase crop yield by 20–30%.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is a multilingual, AI-powered mobile and web-based platform that provides personalized agricultural advisory services to small and marginal farmers. By integrating AI, IoT, and data analytics, the system delivers real-time insights based on soil health, weather patterns, crop stages, and market dynamics.

The platform will act as a digital farming assistant, capable of guiding farmers through the entire crop cycle—from seed selection to post-harvest management—using text, voice, and image-based interactions in regional languages.

Key Features

Personalized Crop Advisory: Suggests crops based on soil type, weather, and land size.

AI-based Pest and Disease Detection: Farmers can upload images for instant pest identification and control advice.

Weather Forecast & Alerts: Provides timely weather updates to prevent crop loss.

Fertilizer & Irrigation Guidance: Recommends optimal usage to reduce cost and prevent soil degradation.

Market Linkage: Displays real-time market prices and connects farmers to nearby buyers or mandis.

Voice & Chatbot Assistance: Helps low-literate farmers interact in their native language through voice commands.


## Technical Approach
Technologies to Be Used

Frontend: Flutter / React Native (for Android & iOS app)

Backend: Python (Flask / FastAPI), Node.js

AI/ML Models: TensorFlow / PyTorch for image recognition (pest detection)

Database: Firebase / PostgreSQL

APIs: OpenWeather API, Soil Health Card Data API, Government Agri APIs

Cloud & IoT: AWS IoT Core / Google Cloud IoT for sensor integration

Language Processing: NLP models (e.g., IndicNLP, Whisper) for multilingual voice and text support

Implementation Methodology

Data Collection: Gather soil, weather, and crop data from government and satellite sources.

Model Development: Train AI models for crop recommendation and pest detection.

Prototype Development: Build multilingual chatbot and mobile interface.

Testing Phase: Pilot with farmers in selected districts to gather feedback.

Deployment: Launch public beta; integrate with state agriculture departments.

Continuous Improvement: Update models and features based on user behavior and outcomes.
## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility

Technically achievable using existing AI, IoT, and cloud technologies.

Can be developed in collaboration with agricultural universities and government data sources.

Open-source libraries reduce development cost.

Potential Challenges

Limited internet access in rural areas.

Resistance to technology adoption among older farmers.

Data quality and regional language translation accuracy.

Mitigation Strategies

Include offline mode and SMS-based alerts.

Conduct training workshops through local extension officers.

Use human-in-loop verification for AI-based advisories initially.
